# Nadi Decision Pack

Nadi Decision Pack is the Phase 1 bridge between research and investor discipline. It turns the currently selected fund, Goal Fit Compass inputs, X-Ray comparison set, watchlist triggers, and the investor's own stated reason into one explainable memo.

## Product Purpose

Retail investors often jump from a fund card to an action without writing down why the fund fits the job. The Decision Pack slows the workflow down. It asks for the intended action, SIP amount, conviction level, next review date, and reason. It then creates a structured pack with:

- Selected fund and intended decision.
- Category, risk band, score, expense, return demo, and drawdown.
- Goal Fit context from the current compass settings.
- X-Ray comparison names already selected by the user.
- Watchlist triggers connected to the fund.
- Evidence checklist and guardrails.
- Copy-ready text and a Journal save action.

## Compliance Posture

The pack is a self-research memo, not personalized advice. It should describe what the user selected, what evidence is visible, and what should be reviewed next. It must not say that the user should buy, sell, switch, hold, or redeem a fund. The pack should continue to use terms such as research, decision support, review, watchlist, evidence, and memo.

## Phase 1 Monetization Fit

The free tier can allow simple local packs for trial usage. Nadi Plus can unlock unlimited saved packs, cloud history after account sync, PDF exports, alert-linked review dates, and citation-backed live evidence once data feeds are connected. This makes the paid tier useful without charging for advice.
