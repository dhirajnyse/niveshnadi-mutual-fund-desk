# Nadi Action Planner

The Nadi Action Planner is the Phase 1 research-to-action layer for retail investors. It converts the selected fund, compare set, Trust Center posture, Investor Readiness Gate, evidence standard, amount being reviewed, conviction, review date, and written reason into a disciplined action memo. The output is intentionally a planning artifact, not a transaction instruction.

The planner separates four useful views: what to do today, blockers, checks before any money moves, and review triggers. This helps a retail investor avoid jumping from a fund score directly to SIP, STP, switch, or continue decisions. It keeps the flow practical while preserving the research-only boundary.

The planner does not store PAN, folio, CAS, bank details, credentials, client identifiers, contact details, or private account notes. For Phase 2 distributor workflows, this boundary must remain separate from ARN, EUIN, client-consent, and suitability records until the regulated workflow is designed and reviewed.
