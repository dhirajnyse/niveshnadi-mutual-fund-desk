# Nadi Behavior Guard

Nadi Behavior Guard is the behavior brake for retail mutual fund research. It is built for the moments when an investor is about to act because of panic, excitement, peer noise, tax pressure, recent performance, SIP fatigue, or a sudden switch urge.

The product principle is simple: many investor mistakes happen between research and action. A fund may be reasonable, but the timing, mood, amount, or reason can still be poor. Behavior Guard slows that moment down and converts it into checks before the user writes a memo.

The guard reads the selected fund, intended action, amount context, mood, trigger, compare-set depth, evidence readiness, risk band, and drawdown. It then creates:

- a discipline score,
- a posture such as pause before action, proceed only with memo, watch discipline, or research can continue,
- four behavior cards covering bias, fund pressure, action friction, and decision rule,
- route buttons to Stress Lab, Cost Reality Lab, Switch Lab, and Decision Pack,
- and a copyable behavior note.

This layer is intentionally not an advice engine. It does not tell the user to buy, sell, switch, redeem, pause, or increase SIP. It says what must be checked before any real-world decision.

Launch guardrails:

- No PAN, folio, CAS, bank data, account credentials, or client identifiers are required.
- Do not show transaction language as a recommendation.
- Add live source citations and updated costs before tying the guard to real fund data.
- Treat tax rush, exit load, redemption, and switch questions as research prompts unless reviewed by qualified compliance and regulatory specialists.
- Keep the guard available in Phase 2 for distributors as an auditable suitability-conversation checklist rather than an automated advice signal.
