# NiveshNadi Brand System

## Name

NiveshNadi means investment river. The name is designed around flows: SIP inflows, STP transfers, SWP withdrawals, portfolio current, and long-term compounding.

## Tagline

Primary: Know the fund before you fund it.

Secondary: Mutual fund research, simplified.

## Visual identity

The logo is a 3D-style letter N shaped like a flowing river. The three streams represent SIP, STP, and SWP. The gold stroke represents long-term compounding and investor prosperity.

## Colors

- Deep ink: trust, research, institutional seriousness.
- River green: investor progress and disciplined flow.
- Research blue: analytics and evidence.
- Saffron gold: India, prosperity, and decision confidence.

## Tone

Simple, evidence-backed, calm, and protective. NiveshNadi should feel like a research companion, not a sales platform.
