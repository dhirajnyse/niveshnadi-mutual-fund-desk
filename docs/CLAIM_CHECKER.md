# Nadi Claim Checker

Nadi Claim Checker is the trust-challenge layer for retail mutual fund research. It is designed for the everyday claims investors hear from advertisements, social media, friends, distributors, blogs, and their own return-chasing thoughts.

The tool checks claim types such as guaranteed returns, best fund, recent winner, low NAV, NFO at Rs. 10 NAV, IDCW income, tax deadline rush, lowest expense, and influencer conviction. It then converts the claim into red flags and research checks.

The feature creates:

- a claim score,
- a posture such as do not rely on this claim, verify before relying, or claim can be researched,
- claim cards for source, selected fund context, and primary concern,
- flag cards for the detected claim patterns,
- route buttons to Evidence Ledger, Compare, Retail Glossary, and Behavior Guard,
- and a copyable claim note.

This is not an advertising validator or advice engine. It does not approve or reject a fund. It challenges the language and forces the investor to inspect evidence, cost, risk, benchmark, source date, and written reason before trusting the claim.

Launch guardrails:

- Do not treat any external claim as true unless source date, citation, risk, cost, benchmark, and evidence are visible.
- Do not accept "guaranteed", "no risk", or "best fund" language as product truth.
- Add official source citations before live use.
- Avoid collecting PAN, folio, CAS, bank data, or account credentials.
- Keep the tool research-only unless the business resolves the applicable SEBI advisory and distributor compliance boundary.
