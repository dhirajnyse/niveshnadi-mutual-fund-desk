# Nadi Clearance Sprint Board

The Nadi Clearance Sprint Board converts memo clearance blockers into a short research route. It is designed for a retail investor who has already screened a fund, compared peers, checked proof gaps, and reached the memo stage, but still needs a practical way to finish the remaining research without rushing into an action.

The board reads the current Memo Clearance Desk posture and builds a small sprint with the first move, next moves, total estimated effort, finish line, and direct workspace routes. If a memo is blocked, the sprint starts with the highest priority blocker. If the memo is already clear in demo mode, it still creates a production-safe route around source refresh, evidence dates, review rhythm, and memo caveats.

This feature is intentionally behavioral. It reduces the chance that a user sees a score and jumps straight to a decision. Instead, it makes the next research task concrete: open the right workspace, resolve the specific gap, rerun clearance, and only then write or copy the decision memo. The sprint can be copied as a research workflow note, but it never becomes advice, suitability approval, or an execution instruction.

For a future live launch, the sprint should be tied to source timestamps, citation status, evidence freshness, riskometer version, TER, benchmark, factsheet, SID/KIM, and portfolio disclosure checks. It must continue to exclude PAN, folio, CAS, bank, contact, account credential, ARN, EUIN, distributor, and client identifiers.
