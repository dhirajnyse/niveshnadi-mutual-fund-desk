# Nadi Daily Command Brief

Nadi Daily Command Brief is the daily research companion for retail investors, introduced in the V66 workflow layer and carried forward into the current desk. It sits near the top of the product and turns the current browser-local state into a practical priority queue: profile readiness, selected fund posture, evidence readiness, compare set, memo reason, review rhythm, alerts, watchlist, and saved research history.

The goal is to reduce confusion when the product has many powerful rooms. Instead of asking the investor to decide where to go next, the Daily Command Brief summarizes the next research checkpoint and gives direct routes to the right workspace. Typical priorities include verifying evidence, adding a peer, writing the decision memo reason, running stress or cost checks, and setting a review rhythm.

## Boundaries

- The brief is a workflow checklist, not investment advice.
- It does not produce buy, sell, hold, switch, or SIP instructions.
- It does not use PAN, folio, CAS, bank, contact, account, credential, ARN, EUIN, or distributor-client data.
- It stays in the Phase 1 self-research boundary and uses demo data plus browser-local workflow metadata only.

Before launch with live data, the command brief should display source dates, data freshness, citation status, and demo/live labels wherever it refers to evidence or performance.
