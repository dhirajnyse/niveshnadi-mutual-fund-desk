# Nadi Research Decision Radar

Nadi Research Decision Radar is the V67 readiness layer for the mutual fund desk. It converts the selected fund, profile route, evidence readiness, compare set, memo status, watchlist, review rhythm, and daily command signals into four simple forces: trust, fit, risk, and discipline.

The purpose is to stop a retail investor from treating one attractive number as a decision. If the score looks usable but evidence is weak, the radar routes the investor to the Evidence Ledger. If fit is weak, it routes back to the Investor Profile Room. If risk is the weak force, it routes to Stress and Cost checks. If discipline is weak, it routes to memo, review, or watchlist rooms before any real-money action is considered.

## Boundaries

- The radar is a self-research posture tool, not investment advice.
- It does not produce buy, sell, hold, switch, SIP, STP, or redemption instructions.
- It does not collect PAN, folio, CAS, bank, contact, account, credential, ARN, EUIN, or distributor-client data.
- It uses demo fund data and browser-local workflow state only.
- Live launch must show source dates, citation paths, demo/live labels, and validation status before the radar is trusted for current-market research.
