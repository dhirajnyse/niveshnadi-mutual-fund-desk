# Founder Beta Operating Room

The Founder Beta Operating Room is the V139 operating layer after the Paid Cohort Expansion Gate. The expansion gate answers whether the beta should widen, hold, or freeze; the operating room answers what the founder actually does during the week. It converts launch readiness into a practical cadence across invite control, payment drills, account rehearsal, support hour, evidence refresh, privacy closeout, compliance wording, and Friday decision review.

The room computes an operating score, posture, invite window, support capacity, priority lane, weekly command queue, freeze rules, and a copyable founder operating packet. It is intentionally narrow because the first paid cohort should be managed as an operating experiment, not as a public launch. If support capacity, payment/refund proof, compliance wording, source evidence, or privacy closeout is weak, the room keeps the posture at founder rehearsal or freeze.

This room protects monetization discipline. Low retail pricing can work only if hidden support, refund, evidence, and account costs are visible before the product invites more users. It also preserves the Phase 1 boundary: no advice, no execution, no PAN, folio, CAS, bank, UPI, card, OTP, ARN, EUIN, distributor client book, or Phase 2 workflow belongs inside the founder beta operating rhythm.
