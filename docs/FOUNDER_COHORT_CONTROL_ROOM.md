# Founder Cohort Control Room

The Founder Cohort Control Room is the V135 operating board for the first retail beta wave. It turns the Founder Invite Proof Path into a controlled cohort ledger so the founder can see whether the product is ready to expand beyond a small rehearsal group.

The room deliberately stays identity-light. It uses cohort IDs and proof states instead of names, phone numbers, PAN, folio, CAS, bank, UPI, card, OTP, nominee, ARN, EUIN, or distributor client records. Each cohort row shows invite status, activation proof, entitlement posture, support outcome, pause or refund readiness, and the next route to clear. This protects the Phase 1 product from collecting sensitive data before the account backend, privacy controls, and support processes are ready.

The room also makes monetization discipline visible. A paid founder cohort should not expand merely because users are interested. It should expand only after activation, support repair, entitlement state, refund and pause handling, export and delete controls, source evidence status, and backend receipt storage are clean. Until those receipts exist, the recommended posture is to hold at a free or founder-reviewed rehearsal cohort.

The next implementation path after this room is backend-owned cohort receipts: persisted invite decisions, activation events, support cases, entitlement state changes, refund or pause events, redaction status, and expansion decisions. That backend layer will move the product from prototype control to launch-grade operating evidence.
