# Founder Invite Proof Path

The Founder Invite Proof Path is the V134 bridge between the Paid Beta Evidence Pack and real user invitations. It answers a practical launch question: if NiveshNadi is ready to test monetization with retail investors, exactly who can be invited, what can be promised, what is blocked, and what evidence must exist before the invitation expands?

The room keeps the first wave intentionally small and reversible. It converts product proof into controlled invitation copy, eligibility rules, access boundaries, support expectations, pause and refund rules, and receipt fields. This keeps the product from sounding like an advisory, execution, or return-promise platform while it is still a founder-reviewed research workflow.

The preferred posture is free founder rehearsal until the payment, account, source, support, refund, and entitlement receipts are strong enough for a paid cohort. If a paid founder cohort opens, it should be capped, manually reviewed, and attached to clear beta language. Users should understand that they are testing saved research discipline: profile route, comparison, evidence check, portfolio X-Ray, decision memo, review date, and exportable research packet.

The invite path also protects privacy. It explicitly excludes PAN, folio, CAS, bank, UPI, card, OTP, nominee, distributor identifiers, and client-book records from Phase 1. It keeps distributor workflows behind the Phase 2 boundary until consent, role-based access, audit logs, and compliance review are ready.
