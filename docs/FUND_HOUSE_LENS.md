# Fund House Lens

Fund House Lens is the Phase 1 v23 stewardship layer for NiveshNadi. It helps a retail investor look beyond recent return and fund name by asking whether the fund house process, evidence, people, cost discipline, AUM capacity, and style stability are clear enough for deeper research.

## What it reviews

- Process clarity from stated style, benchmark, role, and research coverage.
- Evidence depth from mapped sources, holdings fields, factsheet readiness, and citation gaps.
- Cost discipline against the demo sleeve peer average.
- AUM and capacity comfort, with extra caution for capacity-sensitive strategies.
- Manager visibility, tenure readiness, and AMC communication history.
- Style stability using consistency, drawdown, risk band, and portfolio-style language.

## Product role

This is a research discipline layer. It does not rank fund houses as good or bad, does not recommend a fund, and does not tell the investor to buy, sell, switch, hold, pause, or redeem. Its job is to make the next question sharper before the fund enters Watchlist, Compare, X-Ray, or Decision Pack.

## Launch gates

Before live launch, every house-level claim should carry source date, citation path, extraction status, and freshness state. Manager fields should move from demo placeholders to structured AMC factsheet records. Capacity checks should be explicit for mid cap, small cap, flexi cap, thematic, sector, and concentrated strategies.

## Paid tier fit

Fund House Lens can become a Nadi Plus feature through saved house notes, refresh reminders, manager-change alerts, capacity review history, and exportable stewardship packs. The value is workflow memory and evidence discipline, not personalized investment advice.
