# Nadi Fund Selection Funnel

The Nadi Fund Selection Funnel helps a retail investor narrow a large mutual fund universe into a smaller research lane without pretending to recommend a fund. It connects the Investor Profile Room, Category Playbook, Screener, Compare Matrix, Evidence Ledger, and Research Conviction Ladder into one compact workflow.

The funnel has six gates: profile context, category anchor, universe filter, shortlist set, proof check, and decision boundary. Each gate shows whether the research is ready, which metric is driving the status, and where the investor should go next. The goal is to reduce confusion before fund inspection, not to give buy, sell, hold, switch, SIP, STP, or redemption instructions.

## Phase 1 Boundaries

- Uses demo funds and browser-local state only.
- Does not ask for PAN, folio, CAS, bank, contact, account credential, ARN, EUIN, or distributor-client data.
- Narrows categories and candidate funds for research workflow only.
- Requires live AMFI, AMC factsheet, SID/KIM, TER, riskometer, benchmark, and portfolio disclosure proof before production claims are trusted.

## Product Role

Retail investors often start with too many funds and too little structure. The funnel gives them a calmer path: first define context, then anchor category, then filter the universe, then compare a shortlist, then verify evidence, then write the decision boundary. This keeps NiveshNadi useful, disciplined, and clearly separated from personalized investment advice.
