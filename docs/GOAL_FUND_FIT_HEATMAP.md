# Goal-Fund Fit Heatmap

Goal-Fund Fit Heatmap is the Phase 1 v18 selected-fund goal lens for NiveshNadi. It answers a common retail research problem: after a user finds a fund, they still need to understand where that fund may belong and where caution is needed. The heatmap maps the selected fund across emergency cash, short goal, tax saving, first SIP core, long-term wealth, retirement path, income stability, and growth satellite lenses.

The output is intentionally a research map, not a suitability engine. It may say "Primary research lane", "Support research lane", or "Caution lane" because those are workflow labels. It must not say that the fund is suitable for a named investor, that a user should buy it, or that a specific allocation percentage is appropriate. The language should push the user toward goal horizon, cost, evidence freshness, drawdown, overlap, and written-reason checks.

In the prototype, fit scores are derived from demo category, sleeve, risk, Nadi score, and evidence readiness. For launch, the heatmap should connect to live AMFI categories, SID/KIM objectives, riskometer history, TER history, portfolio disclosure dates, and user-entered goal assumptions. The score should be auditable and explainable so users can challenge the route instead of blindly following it.

The copied fit note should include release version, fund name, category, risk band, strongest research lens, each goal-lens score, top checks, and the research-only guardrail. Future paid features can save fit maps across goals after authentication, deletion controls, and privacy controls are complete.
