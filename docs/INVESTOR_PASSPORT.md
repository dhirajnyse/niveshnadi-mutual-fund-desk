# Retail Investor Passport

Retail Investor Passport is the Phase 1 v35 starting layer for NiveshNadi. It helps an individual investor describe the research context before looking at fund names. The inputs stay in the browser and cover the investor need, stage, horizon, monthly SIP capacity, risk comfort, emergency buffer, and decision stage.

The passport does not approve a fund, create a recommendation, or produce a personalized suitability certificate. It converts the inputs into a research posture, category route, suggested workflow lane, and guardrails. The output is meant to reduce impulsive SIP or switch decisions by making the user write the context first.

## What It Routes

- First SIP users toward Research Lanes, Goal Fit, SIP math, and the memo workflow.
- Parking or short-horizon users toward liquidity-first and debt-category research.
- ELSS users toward lock-in, tax fit, and equity drawdown checks.
- Review users toward Portfolio X-Ray, Review Room, Review Vault, and watch triggers.
- Switch users toward Switch Lab, Cost Lab, Stress Lab, and Compare before any change.

## Compliance Boundary

The passport intentionally avoids PAN, folio, CAS, account credentials, transaction history, or distributor identifiers. It is a local self-research guide only. Before launch with account login, live data, payment, or distributor workflows, the security model, data classification, consent language, SEBI boundary, and evidence citations must be reviewed again.
