# Nadi Journey Timeline

The Journey Timeline is the product-level progress rail for Phase 1. It connects the existing research tools into one visible workflow: Profile, Research, Compare, Evidence, Memo, and Review. The timeline reads from current browser-local state such as the Investor Profile Room, selected fund, compare set, evidence readiness, Decision Pack reason, journal count, watchlist, alerts, review vault, and receipt vault.

The timeline is intentionally not a recommendation engine. A completed step means the user has moved through a research discipline checkpoint; it does not mean the fund is suitable, approved, recommended, or ready for execution. The next-step button routes the investor to the first incomplete workflow checkpoint so they can continue research without hunting through the full workspace.

The copyable timeline brief is identity-light. It includes selected fund name, evidence score, compare count, timeline states, and guardrails. It excludes PAN, folio, CAS files, bank information, credentials, contact details, client identifiers, and free-form private financial data. Phase 2 can extend this into distributor/client workflow only after consent, role-based access, audit logging, retention, deletion, and regulatory boundaries are designed.
