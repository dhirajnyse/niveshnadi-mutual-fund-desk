# Market Strategy Room

The Market Strategy Room is the founder-facing strategy layer for NiveshNadi Phase 1. It converts the market conversation into a product surface so pricing, competition, trust posture, and Phase 2 boundaries can be reviewed inside the same workspace as the retail research tools.

The room keeps the B2C posture deliberately light: NiveshNadi Plus should remain around Rs. 100 per month or Rs. 1,000 per year, with a founder offer available while live data, accounts, payment receipts, and support workflows are still being tested. The product should monetize research discipline, saved memory, review cadence, and copyable research packets, not fund pushing or execution.

The competitor view is not copycat analysis. Transaction apps already own execution, research portals already own many data tables, social channels own attention, and MFD tools serve a different operating model. NiveshNadi's wedge is to own the slower moment before investment action: evidence checks, category role, overlap, cost drag, drawdown comfort, written reason, and next review date.

The room also keeps the Phase 2 distributor opportunity visible without mixing it into Phase 1. Distributor pricing can become the larger annual software business only after ARN/EUIN identity, PAN consent, client book boundaries, role-based access, support redaction, audit trails, and compliance review are designed. Until then, the retail product stays a self-research cockpit with clear research-only guardrails.

Launch readiness should improve only when the strategy becomes operational proof: founder beta terms, entitlement receipts, refund rules, support ownership, privacy controls, source dates, citation paths, live-data gates, and account storage are wired beyond browser-only state. The copyable strategy brief gives the founder a clean artifact for investor discussions, partner reviews, and product planning without promising revenue or personalized investment outcomes.
