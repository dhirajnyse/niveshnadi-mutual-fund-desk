# Nadi Memo Clearance Desk

The Nadi Memo Clearance Desk is the Phase 1 bridge between research work and a written decision memo. It does not tell an investor to buy, sell, hold, switch, start SIP, stop SIP, redeem, or execute an STP. Its job is narrower and safer: decide whether the research file is organized enough to draft a memo, or whether the investor should clear a blocker first.

The desk combines the Answer Sheet, Shortlist Reason Board, Proof Gap Queue, Conviction Ladder, selected fund evidence readiness, and visible memo wording. It presents six gates: answer base, shortlist reason, proof gaps, conviction ladder, memo reason, and research boundary. Each gate has a route back to the right workspace, so a weak memo does not become a vague warning; it becomes a specific next action.

For retail investors, the value is behavioral discipline. A fund may look attractive, but the memo should not be treated as ready until the reason is written in the investor's own words, the compare context is clear, the source gaps are visible, and the no-advice boundary is still present. In production, the same desk should require live AMFI, AMC factsheet, SID/KIM, portfolio disclosure, TER, riskometer, benchmark, source date, and citation-path checks before any output is labeled current.

The desk intentionally excludes PAN, folio, CAS, bank, contact, account, credential, ARN, EUIN, distributor, or client identifiers. It is designed for Phase 1 self-research and can later become a controlled handoff gate for Phase 2 distributor workflows.
