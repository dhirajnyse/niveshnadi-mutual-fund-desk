# Nadi Score Anatomy

Nadi Score Anatomy is the score-transparency layer for the selected fund. It explains why a fund has its Nadi score instead of asking a retail investor to trust a black-box number.

## Score Drivers

The demo score is built from five weighted drivers:

- Consistency: 34%.
- Drawdown control: 22%.
- Expense discipline: 18%.
- Research coverage: 16%.
- Return support: 10%.

Return support is intentionally capped so recent performance cannot overpower risk, cost, and consistency. This keeps the research flow away from return-chasing behavior.

## What It Shows

- Raw score for each driver.
- Point contribution from each weighted driver.
- The strongest and weakest score drivers.
- Sleeve peer context.
- Evidence readiness as a separate trust gate.
- Score pressure notes that route users toward Stress Lab, Cost Lab, Evidence Ledger, or peer comparison.

## Product Boundary

Nadi Score Anatomy is a score explanation layer, not a rating, recommendation, suitability approval, or return forecast. A high score does not mean buy, start SIP, switch, or hold. A low score does not mean sell or avoid.

## Launch Gate

Before live launch, every driver should be tied to documented methodology and source-dated data. Evidence readiness must remain visible as a separate citation and freshness gate for AMFI, AMC factsheet, SID/KIM, portfolio disclosure, TER, riskometer, and benchmark sources.
