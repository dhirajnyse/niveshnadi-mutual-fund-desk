# Nadi Signal Strip

Nadi Signal Strip is the first-screen command layer for the selected mutual fund. It is designed to reduce investor confusion at the top of the desk by turning the current fund into a compact research posture, score, evidence status, risk control signal, sleeve peer delta, and next research check.

The strip does not rank funds as advice. It reads the same demo research inputs already used elsewhere in the prototype: Nadi score, evidence readiness, drawdown behavior, expense ratio, sleeve peer context, and compare-set depth. It then decides whether the immediate next action should be Evidence Ledger, Risk Stress Lab, Cost Reality Lab, Fund Compare Matrix, or Nadi Decision Pack.

The key product idea is simple: a retail investor should not be dropped into a large research workspace without a clear first move. The Signal Strip gives that first move while keeping the boundary visible. It says what to inspect next, not what to buy or sell.

Launch requirements before treating this as a live signal:

- Replace demo values with validated AMFI, AMC factsheet, SID/KIM, TER, riskometer, portfolio disclosure, and benchmark feeds.
- Store source date, extraction timestamp, citation URL, and demo/live status next to every signal.
- Keep the output research-only unless the business resolves the SEBI investment adviser and distributor boundary.
- Avoid PAN, folio, CAS, bank, account credential, or client identity fields in the Phase 1 retail strip.

The copy action exports the strip as a short research note that can be pasted into a decision memo, journal, family discussion, or advisor conversation.
