# Paid Beta Evidence Pack

The Paid Beta Evidence Pack is the V133 founder gate for turning NiveshNadi's low-fee retail monetization plan into a responsible launch decision. It sits after the Market Strategy Room because strategy alone is not enough: before retail users are asked to pay, the product needs proof that the paid promise is useful, supportable, reversible, and research-only.

The pack organizes six proof families. Value proof confirms the user can produce a research packet, compare set, Portfolio X-Ray, memo, and review date before paying. Source proof confirms that live-looking claims have source dates, citation paths, freshness rules, and demo/live status. Payment proof checks hosted checkout, webhook signature, invoice, refund, renewal, cancellation, settlement, and entitlement behavior. Account proof checks saved research, export, delete, support lookup, and identity-light backend ownership. Support proof covers failed payment, refund, entitlement repair, invoice lookup, deletion request, and correction handling. Compliance proof keeps the product away from personalized advice, execution, return promises, and distributor-client workflow.

The first cohort should stay small and founder-reviewed. The pack can show Founder Circle at Rs. 499 for the first year and Nadi Plus at Rs. 100 per month or Rs. 1,000 per year, but payment should remain gated by receipts and blockers. Required receipts include pricing version, plan ID, source receipt, saved packet ID, payment gateway event, invoice, entitlement state, support case, redaction status, founder approval, rollback note, and launch window.

This room also protects user trust. It explicitly blocks Phase 1 paid beta from collecting PAN, folio, CAS, bank, UPI, card, or OTP data inside NiveshNadi. The paid promise is saved research discipline, not investment advice or execution. The room is designed to become a copyable founder artifact for launch discussions, payment provider review, support planning, and investor updates.
