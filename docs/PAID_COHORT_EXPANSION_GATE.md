# Paid Cohort Expansion Gate

The Paid Cohort Expansion Gate is the V138 founder go/no-go room for widening the first retail paid beta. It sits after Cohort Decision Replay because expansion should not be driven by excitement, early revenue, or a good-looking screener. It should be driven by proof that paid users can be invited, activated, supported, refunded, downgraded, exported, deleted, and corrected without stale entitlement, hidden support cost, misleading claims, or private-data leakage.

The room combines replay proof, cohort receipt coverage, payment and refund dry-runs, account and vault readiness, support capacity, privacy closeout, compliance wording, live-source posture, and the Phase 2 firewall. It returns a single expansion score, founder decision, cohort cap, top blocker, decision rules, launch packet, and founder checklist. The intended posture is conservative: widen only when the blockers are closed; otherwise stay at a founder rehearsal cap and keep learning from a small number of users.

The gate keeps Phase 1 retail clean. It does not approve investments, collect PAN, folio, CAS, bank, UPI, card, OTP, ARN, EUIN, or distributor client records, and it does not transform the product into an advisory or execution service. It is a product-control surface for when to invite more paying self-research users and what must be fixed before doing so.
