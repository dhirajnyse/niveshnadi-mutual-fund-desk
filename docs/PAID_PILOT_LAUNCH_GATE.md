# Nadi Paid Pilot Launch Gate

Phase 1 v144 adds the Paid Pilot Launch Gate between the Pilot Receipt Vault and Entitlement Bridge. The purpose is to stop the team from treating a working payment demo as permission to charge real users. The gate combines receipt vault replay, payment provider proof, entitlement enforcement, support and refund handling, account route readiness, evidence and claim safety, privacy controls, and founder operating capacity into one go, hold, or no-go decision.

The gate is intentionally conservative. A paid pilot can open only as a tiny founder-reviewed cohort, and only when backend-owned receipts can rebuild checkout, signed webhook, entitlement, invoice, refund, support, settlement, and incident state. A success URL, browser-local flag, screenshot, or manual note cannot unlock paid access. This protects the low retail price from hidden support cost and protects early users from confusing beta access with investment advice or transaction execution.

The room also makes the launch boundary explicit. Phase 1 remains research-only and must not collect PAN, folio, CAS, bank details, UPI credentials, card data, OTPs, gateway secrets, ARN/EUIN, distributor client records, or private note bodies for the paid pilot gate. If support lookup, refund closeout, entitlement replay, settlement reconciliation, source evidence, or public-claim release drifts, the paid pilot should freeze until the weakest gate is repaired and copied into the launch packet.

Phase 1 v145 now places the Backend Ticket Factory immediately after this gate. The gate decides whether the paid pilot is go, hold, or no-go; the ticket factory converts that decision into implementation work with event contracts, acceptance tests, owner routes, blocked-data boundaries, and release dependencies. This keeps the product from treating a strong prototype score as production readiness. The next responsible move is to build and test the ticket stack before widening paid access.
