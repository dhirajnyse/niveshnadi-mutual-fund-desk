# Nadi Proof Gap Queue

The Nadi Proof Gap Queue ranks the missing verification work that remains after the Shortlist Reason Board has classified candidates as Keep, Watch, or Park. It converts scattered gaps into an ordered research task list across evidence, compare, risk stress, cost, category, and decision memo lanes.

The queue exists because retail investors often know a fund looks interesting but do not know what to verify next. It turns each shortlist gap into a priority item with the affected fund, route, urgency, and next workspace. This makes the research journey more disciplined without becoming investment advice.

## Phase 1 Boundaries

- Uses demo mutual fund data and browser-local workflow state only.
- Does not collect PAN, folio, CAS, bank, account, contact, credential, ARN, EUIN, or distributor-client identifiers.
- Ranks verification tasks only; it never tells the user to buy, sell, hold, switch, redeem, start SIP, start STP, or allocate.
- Requires live AMFI, AMC factsheet, SID/KIM, TER, riskometer, benchmark, portfolio disclosure, source dates, and citation paths before production claims are trusted.

## Product Role

The Proof Gap Queue gives the investor a calm operating list. Instead of jumping between screens, the user can clear the most important proof gaps first, then return to the shortlist board and decision memo with a stronger evidence trail.
