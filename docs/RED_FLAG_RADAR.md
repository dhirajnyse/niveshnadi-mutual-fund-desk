# Red Flag Radar

Red Flag Radar is the Phase 1 v19 selected-fund warning layer for NiveshNadi. It gives retail investors a calm way to ask "what could go wrong here?" before they write a decision memo. The radar inspects demo risk band, drawdown, expense versus sleeve peer average, evidence readiness, overlap with the compare set, sector concentration, goal-fit caution zones, and Nadi score comfort.

The feature must remain a research prompt, not a rejection engine and not an approval engine. A red flag should never mean "sell", "avoid", "switch", "buy", or "do not buy". It should mean "finish this specific research check before acting". A calm radar should also not mean that a fund is suitable or safe. It only means that the demo checks did not trigger a major warning.

The radar can add the selected fund to the browser-local Watchlist because that is a workflow reminder. It should not create a transaction workflow. In production, each flag should be backed by live source dates and auditable methods: AMC factsheet, portfolio disclosure, TER history, riskometer, SID/KIM, AMFI category, and benchmark context.

The copied flag note should include release version, fund name, category, risk band, radar posture, watch score, high and review flag counts, individual flag details, suggested research action, and the research-only guardrail. Nadi Plus can later save flag history and review reminders after authentication, deletion, and privacy controls are implemented.
