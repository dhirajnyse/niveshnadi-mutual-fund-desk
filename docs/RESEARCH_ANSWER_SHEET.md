# Nadi Research Answer Sheet

Nadi Research Answer Sheet is the V69 completion layer for retail self-research. It turns the Research Question Stack into a simple writing surface where the investor answers Trust, Fit, Compare, Risk, Decision Memo, and Follow-up prompts in their own words before treating a fund as decision-ready.

The sheet scores answer completeness without pretending to judge whether the fund is good or bad. Thin or missing answers are routed back to the right workspace, such as Evidence Ledger, Profile Room, Compare, Cost, Stress, Decision Pack, Watchlist, or Review Rhythm. The goal is to slow down impulse decisions and help a retail investor build a clear proof trail.

## Local Storage Boundary

Answer Sheet drafts and saved sheets stay in the browser only. They may contain the investor's own research wording, so the interface explicitly warns users not to enter PAN, folio, CAS, bank, contact, account credential, ARN, EUIN, distributor-client, or other sensitive identifiers.

## Boundaries

- The sheet is a research-writing checklist, not investment advice.
- It does not recommend, approve, reject, buy, sell, hold, switch, SIP, STP, redeem, or execute anything.
- Answer quality measures completeness of wording, not suitability or future return.
- Live launch must add source dates, citation paths, extraction confidence, and validation status to any answer that references performance, cost, holdings, risk, or document claims.
