# Nadi Research Briefing

The Nadi Research Briefing is the Phase 1 "brief me first" layer for NiveshNadi. It summarizes the selected fund, compare set, Trust Center posture, Action Planner posture, Investor Readiness Gate, evidence readiness, watchlist triggers, and Review Rhythm queue into one compact research briefing.

The briefing is designed for retail investors who may not know which tool to open next. It separates the output into read-first signals, next research actions, watch items, and pause flags. This keeps the product calm and practical while preserving the research-only boundary.

The briefing can be shaped for self research, family discussion, advisor discussion, or future distributor handoff design. It intentionally excludes PAN, folio, CAS, bank data, credentials, contact details, client identifiers, private account notes, execution instructions, and personalized advice. Phase 2 distributor workflows must remain separate until ARN, EUIN, consent, suitability, and compliance responsibilities are designed.
