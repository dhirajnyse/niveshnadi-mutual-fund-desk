# Nadi Research Conviction Ladder

The Nadi Research Conviction Ladder turns research progress into a simple readiness tier before an investor treats a mutual fund as actionable. It reads the current fund, answer sheet, evidence posture, compare set, risk comfort, memo reason, watchlist, receipts, and review rhythm, then places the work on a six-step ladder: Curious, Researching, Watch-ready, Shortlist-ready, Memo-review ready, and Review-file ready.

The feature is intentionally a workflow discipline layer, not a recommendation engine. It does not say buy, sell, hold, switch, redeem, start SIP, start STP, or approve suitability. Its job is to expose what is still missing: answer quality, source proof, peer review, drawdown comfort, written decision reason, or follow-up discipline.

## Phase 1 Boundaries

- Uses demo research fields and browser-local state only.
- Requires no PAN, folio, CAS, bank, contact, account credential, ARN, EUIN, or distributor-client data.
- Keeps the next action inside the research desk by routing the investor to the next incomplete gate.
- Treats live source dates, citation paths, SID/KIM, factsheets, portfolio disclosure, riskometer, and TER proof as launch gates before any production use.

## Product Role

The ladder gives retail investors a clean language for self-research maturity. Instead of asking whether a fund is "good", the user can ask whether the research is ready. That keeps NiveshNadi aligned with its trust-first posture: evidence before confidence, memo before action, and review discipline before commitment.
