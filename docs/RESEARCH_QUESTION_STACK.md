# Nadi Research Question Stack

Nadi Research Question Stack is the V68 prompt layer for retail self-research. It sits after the Daily Command Brief and the Research Decision Radar, then converts the current fund state into the questions the investor should answer before moving toward any memo or real-money action.

The stack looks at the selected fund, Radar weak spot, evidence readiness, profile fit, compare set, stress and cost posture, decision memo reason, watchlist, review vault, receipt vault, and review rhythm. It then prioritizes six answer-before-action prompts. Each prompt has a route to the right workspace and a proof expectation, such as source map, peer comparison, risk stress result, cost drag check, written reason, or review trigger.

## Boundaries

- The stack creates research questions only; it does not recommend a fund.
- It does not approve, reject, buy, sell, hold, switch, SIP, STP, redeem, or execute anything.
- It does not use PAN, folio, CAS, bank, contact, account, credential, ARN, EUIN, or distributor-client data.
- Questions should remain explainable in plain language so first-time mutual fund investors can use the product without feeling pushed.
- Live launch must attach source dates, citation paths, demo/live status, extraction confidence, and validation status wherever a question refers to current market data.
