# Nadi Research Receipt

Nadi Research Receipt is the proof-of-research layer for NiveshNadi. It gives a retail investor a compact record of what was checked before a watch, shortlist, SIP study, switch study, review, or avoid decision was written down.

The receipt combines the selected fund, Nadi score, evidence readiness, Claim Checker flags, Behavior Guard score, compare-set count, watchlist status, saved alert triggers, review date, and the user's own note. The output is deliberately framed as a receipt, not a recommendation. It proves that a workflow was followed; it does not prove that a fund is suitable or that a return will happen.

The feature creates:

- a receipt score that summarizes research organization,
- a ledger of selected fund, score, evidence, claims, behavior, compare set, and follow-up checks,
- route buttons back to Evidence Ledger, Claim Checker, Behavior Guard, and Decision Pack,
- next proof checks before relying on the receipt,
- and a copyable markdown receipt for journal, family discussion, or future advisor/distributor handoff.

Launch guardrails:

- Do not include PAN, folio, CAS, bank details, credentials, client identifiers, or free-form private notes in exported receipts.
- Do not treat a receipt as personalized advice, tax advice, execution instruction, approval, or guarantee.
- Require live AMFI, AMC factsheet, SID/KIM, portfolio disclosure, TER, benchmark, and riskometer citations before showing live-source confidence.
- Keep the written reason, review date, and evidence boundary visible so a retail user knows what still needs verification.
