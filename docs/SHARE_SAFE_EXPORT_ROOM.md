# Nadi Share-Safe Export Room

The Nadi Share-Safe Export Room is the Phase 1 handoff layer for clean research sharing. Retail investors often want to discuss a fund with family, an advisor, or eventually a mutual fund distributor, but they should not copy private identifiers, account details, note bodies, or transaction language into a casual research pack.

This room reads the selected fund, compare set, Research Memory, Privacy Control posture, and evidence readiness. It then creates a share-safe score, audience boundary, included-items list, scrubbed-items list, and attention checklist. The copyable pack is designed to preserve research context while stripping PAN, folio, CAS, bank details, UPI, card details, demat identifiers, contact details, client identifiers, ARN/EUIN references, private note bodies, execution instructions, advice language, tax guidance, approval wording, and return guarantees.

The export is still local text only. It does not upload files, email anyone, sync to a server, create client records, or approve a mutual fund transaction. For Phase 2, this concept can evolve into a consent-based distributor or advisor handoff flow with role-based permissions, retention rules, audit trails, client-data separation, and explicit privacy notices.
