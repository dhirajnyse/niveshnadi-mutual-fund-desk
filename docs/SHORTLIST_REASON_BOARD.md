# Nadi Shortlist Reason Board

The Nadi Shortlist Reason Board explains why candidate funds are kept, watched, or parked after the Selection Funnel has narrowed the research universe. It combines profile fit, Nadi score, evidence readiness, category match, compare-set inclusion, drawdown pressure, and cost pressure into a simple shortlist workflow view.

Keep, Watch, and Park are deliberately research labels. They do not mean buy, hold, sell, switch, redeem, start SIP, start STP, or allocate money. The board is designed to reduce retail-investor confusion by showing the reason for each candidate's research status and the next proof gap to resolve before any decision memo is trusted.

## Phase 1 Boundaries

- Uses demo mutual fund data and browser-local workflow state only.
- Does not collect PAN, folio, CAS, bank, account, contact, credential, ARN, EUIN, or distributor-client identifiers.
- Routes the user back to Compare, Evidence, Risk, Cost, Category Playbook, or Decision Pack depending on the weakest proof gap.
- Requires live AMFI, AMC factsheet, SID/KIM, TER, riskometer, benchmark, portfolio disclosure, source dates, and citation paths before production claims are trusted.

## Product Role

The board turns a shortlist from a vague emotional choice into a reviewable research artifact. A user can see why a candidate survived the funnel, what still needs proof, and which candidates should be parked without feeling forced into an investment decision.
