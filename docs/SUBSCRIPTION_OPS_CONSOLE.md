# Nadi Subscription Ops Console

The Subscription Ops Console defines the Phase 1 paid-retail operating room before a real checkout or recurring subscription is allowed to affect investor access. It maps renewal operations, failed payment triage, refund and cancellation handling, invoice and tax review, settlement reconciliation, support repair, entitlement repair, and launch-incident closure into one visible runbook.

V114 adds Production Support Tooling so support cases are routed through redacted lookup, case routing, entitlement repair, billing evidence, incident desk, and closeout ownership before a paid beta can widen.

The console is deliberately operational rather than promotional. Each lane has to produce redacted receipts, event timestamps, support-safe notes, and a clear owner before it can be treated as launch-ready. The aim is to prevent a low-cost retail membership from becoming messy once real users start paying Rs. 99/month or Rs. 999/year.

Live launch requires verified payment gateway events, backend idempotency, invoice records, refund policy visibility, support redaction, entitlement repair logs, reconciliation checks, and incident rollback steps. Until those systems exist, the console remains a planning and governance surface only.
