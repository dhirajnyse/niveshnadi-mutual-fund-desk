# Suitability Passport

Suitability Passport is the Phase 1 v20 local profile discipline layer for NiveshNadi. It lets a retail investor enter a simple, browser-only research profile before trusting any fund shortlist. The profile uses time horizon, risk comfort, liquidity need, monthly SIP budget, investor experience, and emergency buffer status.

The feature does not store PAN, folio, CAS, bank, or account data. It does not create a client suitability record. In Phase 1, the passport is a local research lens that helps the user slow down and check whether the selected fund deserves further study.

## What It Scores

- Time horizon versus category volatility.
- Risk comfort versus the fund risk band.
- Liquidity need versus fund sleeve and drawdown behavior.
- Monthly SIP budget versus demo minimum SIP.
- Investor experience versus category complexity.
- Emergency buffer status versus the need to prioritize cash parking.
- Evidence readiness before serious memo use.

## Product Role

The passport sits near the top of the workspace, after Fund DNA and before Goal-Fund Fit Heatmap. This placement is intentional. It helps a user ask "does this fund deserve research for my broad profile?" before moving to goal lenses, red flags, peer benchmarks, stress tests, cost checks, evidence, and decision packs.

## Compliance Boundary

Use "research-compatible", "conditional research", "caution profile", and "profile shortlist". Avoid "suitable for you", "recommended", "best fund for you", "buy", "sell", "switch", "hold", or "redeem".

The top match is only a research starting point. The app must continue to show that every shortlist needs Fit Map, Red Flag Radar, Peer Benchmark Board, Stress Lab, Cost Reality Lab, Evidence Ledger, and Decision Pack before any real-world decision.

## Live Launch Gates

- Confirm that risk-profile questions remain educational unless regulatory posture changes.
- Add source-date visibility for all fund evidence used in the profile score.
- Keep the profile local until authentication, consent, and data retention are designed.
- Do not connect PAN, CAS, distributor client books, or transaction workflows to this feature without updating the security model and compliance notes.
