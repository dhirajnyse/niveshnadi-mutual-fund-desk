# Nadi Trust Center

The Nadi Trust Center is the Phase 1 launch-readiness command layer for NiveshNadi. It combines source evidence, claim release controls, rollback posture, correction notices, data mode, and privacy discipline into one concise trust view.

It is designed to answer a practical product question: can this research surface be trusted for its selected audience, or should it remain in demo, preview, reviewer queue, or launch hold?

V299 connects Trust Center history to the Beta Incident Command Ledger in Backend Audit Receipts. Trust history rows, support-safe wording, monitor windows, reviewer closeout, rollback evidence, production smoke posture, and beta continuation decisions now become command evidence before a beta incident can close.

## Inputs

The Trust Center reads the current selected fund, compare set, evidence readiness, claim release gate, claim release ledger, claim rollback console, correction notice builder, and correction notice ledger.

The user can choose:

- Audience: retail investor, internal reviewer, launch gate, or future distributor desk.
- Scope: selected fund, compare set, public claim surfaces, or full research desk.
- Data mode: demo research data, live-data dry run, launch candidate, or launch hold.
- Tolerance: strict public launch, balanced preview, or prototype demo.

## Outputs

The Trust Center produces:

- Trust score and posture.
- Evidence readiness score.
- Claim release gate score.
- Recovery and rollback control score.
- Privacy boundary score.
- Safe-to-show notes.
- Launch blockers.
- Audit trail count for saved release and correction records.
- Next launch queue.
- Copyable trust brief.

## Postures

Possible Trust Center postures include:

- Launch-ready trust pack.
- Reviewer-ready trust view.
- Demo-safe trust view.
- Trust review queue.
- Launch hold.

The logic intentionally keeps demo data from being treated as live evidence. Strict public launch mode applies penalties when source evidence, release gates, or data mode are not ready.

## Privacy boundary

Trust Center artifacts are product-control records only. They must exclude PAN, folio, CAS, account credentials, bank data, client identifiers, contact data, distributor-client records, and private investor notes.

## Compliance posture

The Trust Center helps organize research-product readiness. It does not recommend a fund, approve a transaction, provide personalized investment advice, approve distributor workflow, provide tax guidance, or guarantee returns.
