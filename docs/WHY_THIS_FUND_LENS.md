# Why This Fund Lens

Why This Fund Lens is the explainability layer for the selected mutual fund. It is designed for retail investors who need to understand a fund in plain language before they compare, stress test, watch, or write a decision memo.

## What It Explains

- The selected fund's plain role in a portfolio research conversation.
- The strongest goal or use-case lens from the Goal-Fund Fit Heatmap.
- What to like based on role clarity, score, consistency, cost, and evidence readiness.
- What to challenge based on red flags, peer cost, overlap, drawdown, and evidence gaps.
- Pause conditions that should stop the investor from rushing into a memo.
- Change-my-mind triggers for future review discipline.

## Inputs

The lens uses the current selected fund, Nadi score, Evidence Ledger readiness, Goal-Fund Fit Heatmap, Red Flag Radar, Peer Benchmark Board, Compare set, shared holdings, and Decision Pack reason state.

## Product Boundary

The lens is an explanation layer, not an investment recommendation. It can say why a fund deserves research, why a user should pause, and what evidence could change the thesis. It must not say buy, sell, hold, switch, start SIP, stop SIP, or approve suitability.

## Launch Gate

Before live launch, all fund claims inside the lens should carry source dates and citations for AMFI scheme identity, AMC factsheet, portfolio disclosure, SID/KIM, riskometer, TER, and benchmark data. Demo data must remain visibly marked until that source layer is connected.
